Santorini!

One of my top two dream destinations, Santorini Greece! Santorini is a beautiful island in Greece known for its white buildings, blue domes, and amazing sunsets overlooking the Aegean Sea.

Tokyo!

My other top dream destination, Tokyo Japan! Tokyo is Japan’s capital and one of the largest cities in the world, famous for modern technology, busy streets, and temples.

Brazil!

Brazil is the largest country in South America and is known for the Amazon rainforest, vibrant culture, and famous Rio de Janeiro beaches.

El Salvador!

El Salvador is a beautiful country known for its volcanoes, surfing beaches, and rich cultural history and is where my mom was born!

Egypt

Egypt is famous for its ancient civilization, including the Great Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, and the Nile River. It is also full of so much history.

London, England

London is the capital of England and is known for Big Ben and Buckingham Palace.

Dublin, Ireland

Dublin is Ireland’s capital city and is famous for its historic streets, traditional pubs, and rich literary history.

Punta Cana, Dominican Republic

Punta Cana is a tropical destination with white sand beaches, beautiful water, and all inclusive resorts.
